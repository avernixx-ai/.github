## What we do

- 🤖 **AI agents** that talk to customers on WhatsApp, web chat and social channels, with a human ready to take over.
- ⚙️ **Workflow automation** for the repetitive jobs: quotations, follow-ups, screening, reports.
- 📊 **Data engineering and BI**, so decisions rest on clean, trusted numbers.
- 🛡️ **AI governance**: controls, audit trails and guardrails, so agents stay explainable and accountable.

## How we work

- **We build, not just advise.** Strategy and working software, from the same team.
- **Governed by design.** Human oversight, clear limits and a record of what the AI did.
- **Privacy first.** Your data stays yours, handled with Singapore's PDPA in mind.
- **Honest about limits.** If something is a demo, we say so.
